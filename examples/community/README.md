## Submit an example

- No Git required: use the [resume example issue form](https://github.com/deepdave98/latex-resume-and-cv-templates/issues/new?template=resume-example.yml).
- Pull request: copy [`TEMPLATE.md`](TEMPLATE.md) to `accepted/early-career-backend-short-topic.md` or a similarly clear name. Create the directory if needed. Open a draft PR; one example per file. Leave the review record for the reviewer.

Issues, PRs, and commit history are public. Redact before submitting: names, contact details, employer and client names, private URLs, and details that identify a customer or incident. Use `[COMPANY]`, `[PRODUCT]`, or `[TEAM]` where needed. Do not attach resumes, screenshots, dashboards, logs, or private proof.

Include what you owned, how you checked the result, and why the rewrite is more precise. A number is optional. If you use one, explain the measurement and baseline safely; label local benchmarks as local. Do not turn a team result into an individual claim.

You must own the wording, have the right to share it, and agree to the [MIT License](../../LICENSE). Choose a name, handle, or **Anonymous** for the published entry. Anonymous hides the byline, not your GitHub account or submission history.

## What happens next

1. A reviewer checks privacy, ownership, and what supports the claim. Questions belong in the issue or draft PR; never upload confidential evidence to answer them.
2. The reviewer proposes an edit. You approve the final wording and byline in a public comment before it is accepted. If the wording changes, you approve it again.
3. The reviewer records that approval, adds the entry to this index, and merges only after the [review checklist](REVIEW.md) is complete.

If context cannot be shared safely, omit it or withdraw the example.

`python3 scripts/check_community_examples.py` checks required sections, review links, permissions, and index coverage. It runs in CI through the unit tests. Drafts fail until their review record and index row are complete. The check cannot verify consent, employment, numbers, or privacy; human review is still required.
