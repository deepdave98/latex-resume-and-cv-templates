Help & privacy

Text: extraction order can differ from the layout. Scans and outlined letters may have no text to copy. This tool does not run OCR or predict how an application system reads your resume.

Pages: a blank-page warning comes from the preview. Very faint content can be missed. Check the original PDF if a preview fails.

Links: destinations are displayed as text. They are never opened or checked online.

Your file: processing runs in this tab. The tool does not upload or save the PDF. Clear, reload, or close the page to discard the review and text edits. Copied text may remain in clipboard history. Saved text files stay in your downloads until you remove them.

If this page is hosted, the host receives the page request, not your PDF. Browser extensions and your operating system are outside this tool’s control.
